Perfect Roll — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026

Perfect Roll is a free, unofficial fan tool for tracking game characters, gear and stash items. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

What we collect

Nothing. Perfect Roll has no accounts on any server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell personal information.

Data you enter

Your characters, gear, stash and notes are stored only on your device, encrypted with the password you choose. They never leave your device unless you export a backup file yourself and choose where to send it. If you forget your password, the data cannot be recovered, because no copy exists anywhere else.

Camera and photos

If you choose to scan an item, the photo is read on your device to find the item's stats and is then discarded. Photos are never uploaded and never stored. The camera is only used when you tap a scan button.

Internet use

The app may download fonts and the on-device text-recognition engine from public content networks the first time they are needed. These requests do not include any of your data. Links to outside websites (such as trading sites) open in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies.

Tips

Optional tips are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles purchases.

Children

Perfect Roll is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on Perfect Roll's Google Play page.
