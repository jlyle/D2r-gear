Perfect Roll — Tester Checklist

Updated Nov 7, 2026 · Jim

Before you start

Perfect Roll is a Diablo II: Resurrected gear tracker for Android. It keeps your characters, their gear and a shared stash, and suggests upgrades for your build. This is the closed test on Google Play, starting with version 1.4.3. Please work through the checks below and tell me anything that breaks, looks wrong or confuses you.

Please stay in the test for at least 14 days. Google only lets me publish the app once at least 12 testers have stayed opted in for 14 days in a row, and it checks that the app is actually being used. Open it a few times a week, even briefly. Leaving the test and rejoining restarts your 14 days.

Join the test and install (Android only)

Use the same Google account for every step: the one signed in to the Play Store on your phone.

  1. Join the tester group. Open groups.google.com/g/perfect-roll-testers and tap Join group. If I added your email to the test myself, skip this step.
  2. Opt in to the test. On your phone, open play.google.com/apps/testing/com.tocfm.perfect_roll and tap Become a tester.
  3. On the same page, tap download it on Google Play, then Install.
  4. Open Perfect Roll. The version number shows next to the name.

Google Play says "Item not found" or the opt-in page has no Become a tester button? Google doesn't see you as a tester yet. Check that you joined the group with the same account the Play Store uses (tap your profile picture in the Play Store to see or switch accounts). Joining can take a few minutes to go through, so wait a bit and open the opt-in link again.

Stay in both. Leaving the group or tapping Leave the program on the opt-in page takes you out of the test and restarts your 14 days.

Updates arrive through Google Play like any other app.

Had an earlier test copy (an .apk file)? Export a backup first (⋯ menu → Export account backup), uninstall the old copy, install from Google Play, then import the backup. Android won't install the Play version over the old copy, and uninstalling erases anything that isn't backed up.

Your data stays on your phone only. Nothing is sent to me unless you send a backup file yourself.

The tip jar doesn't work in the beta. Tapping it shows a popup with some text, but it goes nowhere. That's expected, so no need to report it.

Photos, planning tabs and upgrades

Reading stats from a photo. Point your phone at an item tooltip in the game, straight on, with the whole box in frame. The photo is read on your phone and not kept.

Backup and restore

This matters most: it's how you keep your data when you move to the Play Store version.

Export a backup regularly. Your characters exist only on your phone, so a backup is the only copy if the phone is lost or reset.

General and reporting problems

How to report a problem. Reply to the Reddit post where you found the test (if you know me, a text or email works too). Include:

  1. Your phone model and Android version (Settings → About phone).
  2. What you did, step by step.
  3. What you expected, and what happened instead.
  4. A screenshot if you can.

Thanks for testing.
